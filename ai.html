AI made very simple

AI is just a helper. Here's how to use it.

You don't need to be technical. If you can send a WhatsApp, you can use AI to write, plan and learn faster.

Start here: 4 things to try today

Copy a prompt, paste it into any AI chat app, and fill in the brackets.

1

Fix a tricky message

Get a calm, clear version of an email or text you're nervous to send.

Rewrite this message so it's friendly, clear and short: [paste message]
2

Plan the week's meals

Get a plan and a shopping list in one go.

Plan 5 simple, budget-friendly dinners for [number] people. Then give me one shopping list.
3

Understand anything

Turn confusing letters, contracts or articles into plain language.

Explain this like I'm new to the topic, in 5 bullet points: [paste text]
4

Beat the blank page

Get ideas flowing for a post, a speech or a gift.

Give me 10 ideas for [what you need]. Keep each idea to one line.

The secret to better answers

Good prompts have four parts. Remember R-T-C-F:

Role: who should the AI act as? "You are a friendly fitness coach."
Task: what do you want? "Make me a 20-minute home workout."
Context: what should it know? "I'm a beginner with no equipment."
Format: how should it look? "A simple table with times."
