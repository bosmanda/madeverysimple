Terms of sale, refunds & privacy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Who we are

Made Very Simple (madeverysimple.co.za) is a small South African business in Cape Town that makes colouring books for children. The "Made Very Sweet" books are part of the same shop. You can reach us at info@madeverysimple.co.za.

What you are buying

Our colouring books are sold on this website as printable digital PDF files (A4 size). Nothing is posted to you. Each book has 36 pictures plus a front and back cover. Printed paperback versions of some books are sold separately on Amazon.

Prices and payment

Prices are shown in South African rand (ZAR) and are the full amount you pay. Payments are processed securely by PayFast (card, instant EFT and other methods offered by PayFast). We never see or store your card or bank details.

Delivery

Delivery is immediate. As soon as PayFast confirms your payment, the download buttons appear on screen and we email the download link to the address you gave at checkout. Links work for 7 days and each book can be downloaded 5 times. If the email doesn't arrive within an hour, please check your spam folder or email us and we'll resend it.

How you may use the books

You may print as many copies as you like for your own family, or for one classroom or group you teach. Please don't share, resell, upload or email the files to others. All pictures and text remain the copyright of Made Very Simple.

Refunds

Because the books are digital downloads that can't be returned, we don't offer refunds once a file has been downloaded, unless something is wrong. If a file is faulty, won't open, or isn't what was described, email us within 14 days of your purchase and we will fix the problem, send a new file, or refund you in full. If you paid but never received your download, we'll send it or refund you. Approved refunds are paid back through PayFast to the payment method you used, usually within 7 working days.

Privacy (POPIA)

When you buy, we collect your email address, your first name (if you give it) and your order details. We use them only to deliver your books, to send your receipt and download link, and to help you if there's a problem. We keep order records for as long as the law requires for business records.

We'll only send you news about new books if you tick the box at checkout, and you can unsubscribe at any time. PayFast processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. We never sell or share your information.

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you may ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing info@madeverysimple.co.za. See also our website privacy and affiliate disclosure.
